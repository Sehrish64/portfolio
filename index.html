Sawal Jawab

Seedhe jawab, bina ghumaye.

Website banne mein kitne din lagte hain?
Aam tor par 3 se 7 din — size aur details par munhasir hai. Demo pehle milta hai, taake tum beech mein hi design dekh sako aur tabdeeli bata sako.
Payment kaise hoti hai?
Sirf 50% advance se kaam shuru hota hai (Easypaisa / JazzCash). Baqi raqam tab, jab demo dekh kar tumhein design pasand aa jaye. Pasand na aaye to final payment ka koi dabao nahi.
Kitni tabdeeliyan (changes) milti hain?
Demo stage par tumhari pasand ke hisab se reasonable changes shamil hain — rang, text, tasaveer, sections ki tartib. Maqsad yehi hai ke final website tumhein dil se pasand aaye.
Hosting aur domain ka kharcha kitna hai?
Hosting bilkul free hai (GitHub Pages) — koi monthly fee nahi. Apna domain (jaise .pk ya .com) lena chaaho to taqreeban Rs 3,000 saalana hai; ye ikhtiyari hai, free wale address par bhi website bilkul theek chalti hai.
Kya tum nayi ho? Bharosa kaise karun?
Saaf jawab: haan, mera ye business naya hai — isi liye shuruaati rate bhi kam rakha hai. Bharose ke liye jhooti baatein nahi, mera live kaam upar diya hai: har website khol kar khud dekho, phir faisla karo.
